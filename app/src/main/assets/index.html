    About

    SakayBudget 1.3

    Fares are estimates based on the LTFRB rates reported for September 2026, plus fares you reported yourself. Distances are approximate, so what the conductor charges can differ by a peso or two. Your budget, trips and settings are saved on this device, and the Android app works without internet.

    How fares are worked out

    Fare rules follow the LTFRB rates reported for September 2026: traditional jeepney ₱14 for the first 4 km then ₱2 per km, modern jeepney ₱17 then ₱2.40 per km, ordinary city bus ₱15 for the first 5 km then ₱2.49 per km, air-conditioned city bus ₱18 then ₱2.98 per km, and provisional UV Express rates of ₱2.60 (traditional) and ₱3.00 (modern) per km. Discounted fares are 20% off, rounded up to the next peso. Distances are estimated from approximate map positions, so a conductor’s fare may differ; type the km if you know it. Places come from the Metro Manila bus routes and jeepney and UV Express routes lists.
